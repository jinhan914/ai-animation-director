Type a script.
Director casts the animation.

Most tools ask you to pick a library first. Director does the opposite: it reads your script, splits it into scenes, and picks the right animation library for every scene — then generates the animation and plays it back in your browser.

01 — WRITE

Script in, scenes out

One idea per scene. Director splits on blank lines, headings, or sentences — and times each scene from its word count.

02 — CAST

The right library, per scene

Data gets a diagram engine. A formula gets Manim. A launch gets particles. Every choice comes with a plain-language reason.

03 — PLAY

Preview is the product

Watch the whole cut, scrub the timeline, re-cast a scene, or edit the script and regenerate. The code is the source of truth.

How it works

    
Director's casting rules come from a systematic study of 18 free, open-source animation and video libraries — when each one fits, and when it does not.

The full production pipeline (Remotion for narrated video, Manim for maths, Motion Canvas for diagrams) is documented in the repo README.
